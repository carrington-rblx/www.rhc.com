Understand your coverage options

Medicare has different parts and ways to receive coverage. Learn the basics before making a decision.

Original Medicare

Includes Part A (hospital insurance) and Part B (medical insurance). You can generally use any doctor or hospital that takes Medicare.

  • Learn what Medicare covers ↗
  • Look up covered services ↗

Medicare Advantage (Part C)

Private companies approved by Medicare offer these plans as an alternative way to receive Part A and Part B benefits. Many include drug coverage.

  • Compare coverage choices ↗
  • Compare plans in your area ↗
