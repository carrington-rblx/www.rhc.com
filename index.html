Your resource center

A good place to start.

Use these practical checklists to prepare for a healthcare conversation. They are general educational tools, not medical or insurance advice.

Prepare for an appointment

A few simple things to gather before speaking with a healthcare professional.

Write down your main concern, current medications and supplements, allergies, recent changes, and the questions you want answered. Bring relevant records if you have them. If something is unclear, ask the clinician to explain it in everyday language.

Understand your options

Questions that can help you compare choices and understand what is involved.

Ask what each option includes, what it may cost, what is not included, what alternatives exist, who to contact with questions, and what happens next. Take notes and request written information when available.

Organize your health information

Keep the essentials together so you can find them when you need them.

Keep an up-to-date medication list, provider contact details, important health history, allergies, and copies of relevant documents in a secure place. Share sensitive information only through trusted, appropriate channels.

Know when to seek urgent help

Do not wait for a website or message if you may be experiencing an emergency.

For a medical emergency, contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. Rossi HealthCare's website is not an emergency service and is not monitored for urgent medical concerns.
